How this page works

Operational Degraded / slow Down No data

Every component is probed live from your browser the moment this page loads, and again automatically every 60 seconds. A check is marked degraded when it responds slower than 2 seconds, and down when it errors or times out (10 seconds). The 90-day uptime strip is built from check history stored locally in this browser, so it fills in over time as you revisit. Seeing a problem that isn't shown here? Email hello@impactmojo.in.

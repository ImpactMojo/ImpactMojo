What these figures are

  • SAJC says its editions are not exhaustive: they record what was reported and could be verified. ImpactMojo has not checked the reports behind each record, and nothing here goes beyond what SAJC and its sources state.
  • A record is one source-linked sentence. One incident can appear in several sections or editions, so do not add records up as incidents.
  • A date is shown only where the sentence states one inside the edition's own period. Records without one are marked undated, and the state is read from the place names in the sentence.
  • Where a sentence describes sexual violence or someone under 18, its wording is withheld here. The count and the link to the report stay.
  • The record wording is SAJC's. The state outlines are from DataMeet's Census 2011 districts (CC BY 2.5 IN).

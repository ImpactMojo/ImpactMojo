Marginalia

Cartoon essays on development work — the sector’s claim, the field’s reply, and a footnote to back it up. Five essays, fifty drawings, and counting.

Every cartoon runs the same setup: someone official says the polished thing the development sector likes to say, and someone who does the real work — a field officer, an ASHA, a farmer, a beneficiary — says what usually goes unsaid. The jokes are drawn; every footnote is real. A companion in spirit to the Between the Logframes podcast.

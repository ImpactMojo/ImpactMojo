RCT Readiness Diagnostic
Before you commit to an expensive randomised trial, work through five honest checks: where your evidence really sits on the hierarchy, whether your intervention and theory of change are stress-tested, whether your rollout can support ethical randomisation, whether your baseline has room to detect real change (the ceiling/floor trap), and whether an RCT is even the right tool. Research & Methods
Why City Boundaries Lie
An interactive lesson on why administrative city borders don’t match the real, economic city — Noida thriving on Delhi’s edge but officially separate, Dhaka’s limits swallowing farmland while missing dense settlements. Toggle a schematic map between administrative and economic boundaries, see how India’s Census rule undercounts its urban population, and use a mini-classifier to apply the 2-of-3 satellite rule (population, built-up, night-lights) that redraws cities from space. Then record which boundary your own analysis will use and export a boundary-decision note for your methods section. Hands off to Development Data Studio’s open Global Urban Boundaries dataset.

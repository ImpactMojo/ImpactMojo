◇ Sampling Toolkit

Sampling, done right

Two free tools that take you from "how many should we survey?" to a defensible sampling plan — first learn the ideas, then design your study. Built for MEL practitioners across South Asia.

Level 1 · Learn the ideas → Level 2 · Design your sample
Level 1 · Primer

Sampling Basics Free

New to sampling? Start here. A plain-language, no-maths, ten-minute grounding in the ideas — with two quick interactive demos.

  • Population vs sample; why we don't measure everyone
  • Bias & representativeness (convenience-vs-random demo)
  • Margin of error & confidence (a sample-size slider)
  • Clusters, describing vs comparing, a plain glossary
Start with the basics →
Level 2 · Studio

Sampling Design Studio Free · export Premium

Already know the basics? Design a real, defensible sampling strategy and size it properly — with the maths shown and explained as you go.

  • Recommends a strategy — SRS, stratified, cluster, multistage
  • Sizes proportion, mean and impact (power/MDE) designs
  • ICC library, cluster cost optimiser, live self-validation
  • Printable plan & a donor/IRB "defend this number" note
Open the studio →

Which do I need?

• Never designed a sample before? Start with Sampling Basics — ten minutes, no maths.
• Know the concepts and need a real number and plan? Go straight to the Sampling Design Studio.
• Just want a quick sample size? The Studio does that too — pick your question and read off the number.

About this course

How movements form, act, and change society: theory grounded in South Asian practice

A rigorous, South Asia-first flagship on how social movements form, act, and change society. Movement theory (Tilly, Tarrow, Sharp, Chenoweth) is grounded in South Asian cases: Chipko, Narmada, anti-caste and Dalit assertion, the farmers' protest, Shaheen Bagh, Sri Lanka's Aragalaya, and the 2024–26 Gen Z wave.

  • 8+ Movement Cases

# Practice Packs Guide

## What Are Practice Packs?

Practice Packs are **short, interactive job-to-be-done sprints** for development practitioners: 4 modules, about 3 hours, one concrete deliverable. Each pack is a working tool, not just reading material: your inputs auto-save in the browser, and a capstone builder assembles them into a finished artefact you can export.

ImpactMojo offers **18 Practice Packs in two tracks**: 9 Subject Packs (designing rigorous evaluation in a specific development domain) and 9 Method Packs (cross-cutting toolkit skills applicable across every domain). The **first two modules of every pack are free with no login**; the full pack, modules 3–4 plus the capstone builder, is unlocked with Premium (₹399/mo, all packs) or a one-time ₹299 purchase per pack.

## The Practice Pack Contract

Every pack follows the same structure, so you know what you are getting before you commit:

1. **4 modules, ~25 minutes each**: a short read, a worked example, an interactive form, and a self-check question (MCQ) per module
2. **~3 hours total**: roughly 2 hours of reading plus 1 hour of exercise; fits one afternoon or four short sittings across a week
3. **A live capstone**: your module answers auto-populate the final artefact as you work; click "Build my brief" and export as markdown or print/PDF
4. **Auto-save, no login**: inputs persist in your browser's localStorage between visits; nothing is transmitted anywhere

## How Educators Can Use Practice Packs

### As Structured Assignments

The capstone artefact is a natural graded deliverable: assign the ToR Writing pack and collect the exported ToR, or the Logframe pack and collect the logframe. The module structure means learners arrive at the artefact through guided steps rather than a blank page.

### As Team Upskilling Sprints

Run one pack per fortnight with an M&E or programme team: modules 1–2 individually (free tier), then a working session to complete modules 3–4 and build the capstone together against a live programme. The output is a real document the team needed anyway.

### As a Bridge Between Courses and Real Work

A natural sequence: course (concepts) → lab (practice) → Practice Pack (a finished, exportable artefact for a specific job-to-be-done). For example, the MEL course, then the MEL Plan Lab, then the MEL from Scratch pack for a new posting.

### For Domain-Specific Cohorts

Subject Packs meet learners where their brief starts: "we need an SEL evaluation," not "we need a survey instrument." Run the matching Subject Pack with a domain cohort first, then deepen individual skills with Method Packs.

## Tips

- **Pick by the deliverable, not the topic.** Every pack card states exactly what you walk out with: choose the pack whose artefact matches your next deadline.
- **Your answers stay in your browser.** Auto-save uses localStorage, so use the same browser and device across sittings, and export before clearing browser data.
- **Do the self-checks honestly.** The MCQs at the end of each module are calibrated to the mistakes practitioners actually make: a wrong answer is a signal to re-read, not to guess again.
- **Subject first, then Method.** Once you have done two or three Subject Packs you will know which skills need deepening: that is what the Method Packs are for.

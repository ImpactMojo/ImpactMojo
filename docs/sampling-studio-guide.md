# Sampling Studio & Sampling Labs Guide

## What Is the Sampling Studio?

The Sampling Studio is ImpactMojo's set of browser-based tools for **learning sampling and then designing a real one** &mdash; from the intuition behind a sample size to a field-ready sampling strategy you can defend to a funder or an ethics board.

It is **free to use, in the browser, with no login**. Only exporting your finished plan (and a facilitator mode for training) is Premium.

The tools live in the Labs and are gathered under one hub, the **Sampling Toolkit** ([/Labs/sampling-toolkit.html](/Labs/sampling-toolkit.html)) &mdash; "learn it, then design it".

---

## The Three Pieces

### 1. Sampling Basics &mdash; the primer

[Sampling Basics](/Labs/sampling-basics-lab.html) is a plain-language primer: what a sample is, why you can learn about a population from a fraction of it, the difference between probability and non-probability designs, and the everyday traps (convenience samples, non-response, and the false comfort of a big but biased sample). Start here if the word "sampling" makes you reach for a statistician.

### 2. Sampling Design Studio &mdash; the builder

[Sampling Design Studio](/Labs/sampling-design-lab.html) is the interactive heart of the toolkit. It walks you through the decisions that fix a sample size, one question at a time:

- **What do you need to measure?** &mdash; the outcome and its type (a proportion, a mean, a change).
- **How far does your programme reach?** &mdash; the population you're generalising to.
- **Whose responses will you measure?** &mdash; the unit and any clustering.
- **How precise must the answer be?** &mdash; confidence, margin of error, and the power to detect the change you care about.

From your answers it builds **your sampling strategy** &mdash; the recommended design and the sample size &mdash; and a **sample-size justification** you can paste straight into a proposal or a ToR.

### 3. Sampling Toolkit &mdash; the hub

The [Sampling Toolkit](/Labs/sampling-toolkit.html) ties the two together, so you can move from the primer into the builder without hunting for the next step.

## Free vs Premium

Everything above &mdash; the primer, the full builder, and seeing every recommended sample size &mdash; is **free, no login**. A [Premium](/premium.html) plan unlocks two things:

- **Export** &mdash; download or print the ready-to-field sampling plan and its justification.
- **Facilitator mode** &mdash; run the Design Studio as a teaching tool with a group.

This is the same "free to use, pay to export" model as ImpactMojo's other pro tools (see [The Freemium Model](freemium-and-premium-guide.md)).

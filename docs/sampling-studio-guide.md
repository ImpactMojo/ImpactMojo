# Sampling Design Studio guide

## What it is

The Sampling Design Studio is ImpactMojo's browser-based tool for **learning sampling and then designing a real one**, from the intuition behind a sample size to a field-ready sampling strategy you can defend to a funder or an ethics board.

It is **free to use, in the browser, with no login**. Only exporting your finished plan (and a facilitator mode for training) is Premium.

It is one page, the [Sampling Design Studio](/Labs/sampling-design-lab.html). Until 2026-10-12 the primer was a separate page (Sampling Basics) and a third page (the Sampling Toolkit) linked the two; both now redirect into the Studio.

---

## The two parts of the Studio

### 1. Start here: the basics

The Studio opens with a closed section headed "New to sampling? Read the basics first". It is a plain-language primer of seven short sections: why we sample, representativeness and bias, why random beats big, margin of error and confidence, clusters and the design effect, describing against comparing, and a glossary of every term the steps use. Two demonstrations run in the browser: a convenience sample against a random one drawn from the same village, and a slider that shows the margin of error shrinking with sample size. A link to [/Labs/sampling-design-lab.html#basics](/Labs/sampling-design-lab.html#basics) opens it directly.

### 2. The five steps

Below the primer, the Studio walks you through the decisions that fix a sample size, one question at a time:

- **What do you need to measure?** The outcome and its type (a proportion, a mean, a change).
- **How far does your programme reach?** The population you're generalising to.
- **Whose responses will you measure?** The unit and any clustering.
- **How precise must the answer be?** Confidence, margin of error, and the power to detect the change you care about.

From your answers it builds **your sampling strategy** (the recommended design and the sample size) and a **sample-size justification** you can paste into a proposal or a ToR.

## Free and paid

Everything above (the primer, the full builder, and seeing every recommended sample size) is **free, no login**. A [Premium](/premium.html) plan unlocks two things:

- **Export** &mdash; download or print the ready-to-field sampling plan and its justification.
- **Facilitator mode** &mdash; run the Design Studio as a teaching tool with a group.

This is the same "free to use, pay to export" model as ImpactMojo's other pro tools (see [The Freemium Model](freemium-and-premium-guide.md)).

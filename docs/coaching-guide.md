# Coaching Guide

## What Is Coaching?

Coaching is ImpactMojo's **paid one-on-one guidance and consultation service**, delivered directly by the platform's founding team. Where the courses, games, and handouts are free self-study, Coaching is personal, scheduled time with an expert: for your career, your research design, or your data.

Every service is offered on a **sliding scale** based on your circumstances, because the goal is to keep quality guidance accessible to everyone working in the development sector.

Browse and book at [/coaching.html](/coaching.html).

---

## Who It's For

Coaching is aimed at **development-sector professionals**: people navigating a career in the impact space, designing field research, or working through a dataset. It suits both individuals and teams. Whether you are transitioning careers, planning a rigorous study, or trying to make sense of survey results, the sessions are one-on-one and tailored to what you bring.

**Priority Access:** signed-in members on the **Professional** or **Organization** subscription tier see a Priority Access badge, their booking requests are prioritized for faster response times.

## The Services

Three one-on-one services are offered, each with a listed price and a sliding-scale range:

- **Development Career Counselling**: 60 minutes, 1-on-1. Personalized advice on career transitions, skill development, and professional growth in the impact space. **₹1,500** (sliding scale: **₹400 – ₹2,000**).
- **Research Design Consultation**: 90 minutes, includes notes. Consultation on research methodology and sampling strategies, field studies, survey instruments, and qualitative research planning. **₹2,500** (sliding scale: **₹1,000 – ₹3,000**).
- **Data Analysis Consultation**: 90 minutes, hands-on help. Support with cleaning datasets, choosing methods, interpreting results, and building data stories. **₹2,000** (sliding scale: **₹800 – ₹2,500**).

## Three-Day Intensive Workshops

Beyond the one-on-one sessions, Coaching offers **three-day intensive workshops**: a deep dive into any ImpactMojo course topic, for teams or individuals wanting comprehensive training in a specific area.

- **Duration:** 3 days (6 hours/day).
- **Price:** **₹6,000** (sliding scale: **₹3,000 – ₹8,000**).
- **Topics:** pick from any of the platform's 101-course topics on the page, from Development Economics 101 and Gender Studies 101 to MEAL 101, Econometrics 101, and Data Feminism 101.

## How to Book

- **One-on-one sessions:** click the **Book an appointment** button on any service card to schedule directly through Google Calendar.
- **Workshops:** there is no direct calendar booking, use the [Contact page](/contact.html) and mention your preferred course topic and dates.
- **Not sure which service fits?** Use the "Have Questions?" contact call-to-action, write to `hello@impactmojo.in`, or ask **Mojini**, the on-page assistant, for pricing, service, and booking help.

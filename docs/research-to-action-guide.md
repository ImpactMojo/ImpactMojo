# Research to Action Guide

## What Is Research to Action?

Research to Action is ImpactMojo's **visual poster album**: **7 series of 10 posters each (70 posters)** on turning research into better decisions, stronger action, and real-world impact. Each poster distils one idea into a single, shareable page, designed for workshops, classrooms, and team walls.

Every series forms a sequence, but each poster also stands on its own. The album is **free, browser-based, and requires no login**, and posters are free to share under the platform's usual CC BY-NC-ND terms.

## How the Album Works

1. **Series switcher**: tabs at the top let you pick any of the seven series; each tab shows the series name, sub-theme, and poster count
2. **Spotlight stage**: the selected poster displays large, with its title and a one-line blurb; arrow buttons (or the left/right keyboard arrows) move through the series
3. **Filmstrip**: a scrollable thumbnail strip below the stage lets you jump to any poster in the current series
4. **Full-size view**: click any poster (or "View full size") to open it in a lightbox for projection or close reading
5. **Download**: every poster has a Download button that saves the full-resolution JPG with a descriptive filename, ready for printing or slide decks

### Deep-Linking

The URL hash tracks your position as `#series-poster`. For example, `/research-to-action/#4-3` opens Series 4 (Research Methods), Poster 3 (Survey Design: Ask Better Questions). A bare number like `#3` opens that poster in Series 1. Use these links in syllabi, emails, and workshop agendas to send people to an exact poster.

### Sharing and Licensing

Posters are free to use in trainings and programmes under the platform's CC BY-NC-ND terms: share them unmodified, with attribution, for non-commercial purposes. Downloaded files carry descriptive names (e.g. `impactmojo-research-methods-03.jpg`) so a folder of posters stays organised.

## How Educators Can Use the Posters

### As Poster Walks

Print a full series (10 posters), put them up around the training room, and run a gallery walk: participants circulate in pairs, pick the poster that most challenges their current practice, and report back. Series 6 (Home Truths) is especially effective for this: every poster names a sector habit people recognise.

### As One-Poster-Per-Week Staff Learning

Share one poster a week on the team notice board or messaging group, with a single discussion question. A ten-week series becomes a low-cost, zero-preparation staff learning cycle: Series 2 (Data to Decisions) works well for M&E teams, Series 7 for programme design teams.

### As Session Openers and Closers

Open a training session by projecting the relevant poster full-size from the lightbox; close by returning to it and asking what participants would now add or challenge. One poster, one idea, one conversation.

### As Handout Companions to Courses

Posters pair naturally with ImpactMojo courses and labs: Series 3 alongside a MEL course, Series 4 alongside the Survey Design Lab, Series 5 alongside the Climate Risk & Adaptation Lab. The poster is the memorable one-page summary; the course is the depth.

## Getting Started

1. **Browse the album** at [/research-to-action/](/research-to-action/) and click through a series with the arrow keys
2. **Start with Series 1**: it sets up the research-to-action arc that the other series build on
3. **Download a poster** you would put on your own wall: the print test is a good relevance test
4. **Bookmark deep-links** (e.g. `#6-4` for "Pilots Are Not Proof of Scale") for the posters you cite most often
5. **Plan a series into a calendar**: ten posters map neatly onto a ten-week team learning cycle or a term of weekly seminars

## Tips

- **Each poster stands alone.** You do not need to use a whole series: pull single posters into decks, reports, and workshops as needed.
- **Print at full size.** The downloads are high-resolution square images that print cleanly at A3 for training-room walls.
- **Use the blurbs as discussion questions.** Each poster's one-line blurb is already a provocation: read it aloud and ask "is this true of our programme?"
- **Suggest a series.** Have a topic you would like to see as a future series? Use the contact form at [/contact.html](/contact.html): the album is a living collection and more series are on the way.

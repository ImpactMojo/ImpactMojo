# Assessment Series Guide

## What Is the Assessment Series?

The Assessment Series is a set of **500-question multiple-choice banks**, one per subject area, each delivered as a **branded, print-ready PDF** with a **full answer key**. It is the testing layer for ImpactMojo: where the courses, labs and games teach, the Assessment Series lets you check what has actually landed.

Each bank holds roughly **500 original MCQs**, organised into sections, with exactly four options per question, one defensible correct answer, and a one-line explanation. It is built for trainers, examiners and self-testers who need real coverage, not the same handful of recycled questions.

Each bank costs **₹499** and is a standalone purchase. Buy the one that fits your work, or several.

## What's Inside a Bank

Every bank follows the same shape, so you know what you are getting:

- **~500 multiple-choice questions**, grouped into sections.
- **Exactly four options** per question, one correct, with plausible distractors.
- A **compact answer key**, plus a separate **answer key with one-line explanations**.
- **Varied difficulty**: recall, application and analysis.
- A **print-ready, branded PDF** you can reuse across your team and cohorts.

Use a bank to build a pre/post test, a certification exam, a weekly quiz, or simply to check your own gaps honestly.

## How You Buy and Receive It

Delivery is deliberately simple and works over UPI:

1. **Pay ₹499 by UPI**: scan the QR on the product page or send to the UPI ID shown (`impactmojo@ibl`). You can also order on WhatsApp.
2. **Confirm your order**: fill the short form on the product page with your name, email and the UPI transaction reference.
3. **Receive the PDF**: once the payment is verified, the file is emailed to you, usually well within **24 hours**.

Buy once and use it across your own and your organisation's cohorts, exams and quizzes. Refunds follow the site [refund policy](/refund-policy.html); questions go to [contact](/contact.html).

## How It Fits the Freemium Model

ImpactMojo's **courses, labs and games are free**: that is the teaching. The Assessment Series is a small **paid layer** for people who need a large, ready-made set of accurate questions and don't have a week to write them.

Think of it as pairing: the free courses do the teaching, the ₹499 banks do the testing. Nothing behind the paywall is required to learn on ImpactMojo: the banks exist for trainers and examiners who want coverage and rigour off the shelf.

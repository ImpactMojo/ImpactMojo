# Build Circles Guide

## What Are Build Circles?

Build Circles are **small, four-week AI build cohorts for monitoring and evaluation (M&E) work**. Each circle is a peer group of **6–10 builders**, matched by experience, who spend four weeks turning one real M&E problem into a **working AI workflow they demo**.

There are no lectures and no fixed curriculum. A circle runs on a **weekly 60-minute sync**, a **WhatsApp group** for nudges and unblocking, and a **Demo Day** at the end where your build speaks for itself. The guiding principle is simple: **"real enough to demo" beats polished.**

Build Circles are facilitated by **Dr. Varna Sri Raman & Vandana Soni**, ImpactMojo's founders. They match circles by experience, keep the weekly syncs sharp, and unblock builders: they do not lecture.

---

## Who It's For

Build Circles are for **M&E professionals, MEAL teams, NGO programme staff, consultants, researchers, and students** who want a working AI workflow rather than another certificate of attendance.

No coding is required. Most builders use no-code tools and AI assistants, and the circle helps when someone is stuck. **Teams are welcome too**: one deposit per person, and each person demos (or shares the team demo) to get their deposit back.

---

## How a Circle Works

Circles have **rolling starts**: yours begins when a group at your level fills. Sessions are typically **weekday evenings IST**, with the exact schedule agreed on WhatsApp. Expect **roughly 4–6 hours a week**: one 60-minute sync plus your own build time.

The four-week rhythm:

- **Week 0: Kickoff.** Intros, pick your problem, choose your tools. Leave with a plan you can start on tomorrow.
- **Week 1, Frame it.** Post your project framing: the problem, who it's for, and what "working" looks like in four weeks.
- **Weeks 2–3: Build and test.** Build your workflow, then test it with peers or real users. The circle unblocks you on WhatsApp between syncs.
- **Week 4: Demo Day.** A 5-minute demo plus 3-minute Q&A, recorded. Demo, and your full deposit comes back: plus a verified certificate.

## The Deposit

The deposit is the heart of Build Circles: **skin in the game, returned in full the moment you show up and demo.**

- **Joining the waitlist is free**: no payment at signup, ever.
- When enough builders at your level are ready, ImpactMojo confirms dates on WhatsApp and only then asks for a **₹1,000 seat deposit via UPI** (payment note: *"ImpactMojo Build Circle Deposit"*).
- You build for four weeks.
- **Demo on Demo Day and the full ₹1,000 comes back**, plus a [verified ImpactMojo certificate](/verify-certificate.html). A rough-but-real demo counts. No demo, no refund.

There is no course fee: you pay only the refundable ₹1,000 deposit, and only after your circle is scheduled.

## Pick a Real Problem

Every builder works on **one real problem**. You can bring your own from work, or pick from the **15 [ImpactMojo Live Case Challenges](/challenges.html)**: for example, redesigning a flawed logframe, critiquing a programme dashboard, auditing an AI model for bias, running a GESI audit of a livelihood programme, building a Power BI dashboard from messy data, or drafting a policy memo before the window closes.

---

## Your Stack, Your Call

You choose your own tools. Common picks include:

- **No-code:** n8n, Make
- **AI assistants:** Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini
- **Data:** Open Government Data India, or your own programme data
- **Channels:** WhatsApp, forms, email

ImpactMojo's free tools (the Advisory Board preview, labs, and MOJINI) are available throughout. Whatever you build is **yours**; builds are showcased on ImpactMojo only with your permission.

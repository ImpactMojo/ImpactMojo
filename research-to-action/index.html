About these posters

Each poster distills one idea into a single, shareable page — designed for workshops, classrooms, and team walls. Every series forms a sequence, but each poster also stands on its own. The seven series so far: Research to Action (question to impact), Data to Decisions, Evaluation in Practice, Research Methods (without panic), Climate, Health & Resilience, Home Truths (development-sector realities), and Policy & Programme Design. More series are on the way.

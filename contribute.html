Contribute

Share your practitioner expertise with the South Asia development community. Author a course, a deep dive, a lab or a handout — free and open-licensed, published under your name, with our editorial support.

Ways to contribute

Pick the format that fits what you want to share — from a full flagship course to a single printable handout. Not sure? Propose an idea and we'll help you place it.

Flagship course

A deep, modular course (10–15 modules) on a subject you know cold — the platform's most substantial format.

Biggest lift · highest impact

101 course

A self-paced, ~100-slide interactive deck that introduces one topic or method clearly and from scratch.

Focused · self-contained

Deep Dive

A curated, fully-cited reading path on a live development question — the best sources, annotated, in order.

Research & synthesis

Data Note

An independent data investigation — take one public dataset, chart it, and show what the numbers do (and don't) prove.

Analysis & charts

Interactive lab

A hands-on, browser-based tool that lets practitioners do something — we help with the build.

Design the logic, we build

Reading companion

An interactive companion to a key development text — the ideas, the arguments, and how to interrogate them.

One book, done well

Handout

A printable one-pager or quick-reference card on a concept, method or checklist practitioners reach for.

Smallest lift · quick win

How it works

1

Propose

Submit the form below with your idea, your background, and a rough module outline. No polished draft needed yet.

2

Editorial review

Our team reviews your proposal for quality, accuracy, and fit with the platform, and gets back to you.

3

Build together

Approved authors work with us to format the course on our standard template, with editorial support.

4

Publish

Your course goes live free for learners under CC BY-NC-ND, with full author credit on every page.

Contributor guidelines

  • Who can contribute: practitioners, researchers, and educators with real field or domain experience in development, social impact, or allied fields.
  • What makes a good course: evidence-based and practical, grounded in South-Asian realities where relevant, and useful to people doing the work — not just theory.
  • Licensing: courses publish under Creative Commons CC BY-NC-ND, with clear attribution to you as the author. You keep credit; learners get it free.
  • Always free to learners: ImpactMojo never charges learners for course content. Contributing is a way to give back to the community.

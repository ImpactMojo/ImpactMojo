About these maps

  • The Content Map is generated live from the platform's search index, so it always reflects what's published.
  • CSR in India is built from real figures: national CSR spend by sector and the actual FY2023-24 CSR of the ten biggest corporate spenders, sized by rupees. See its notes for exactly what's measured vs. documented.
  • The School-Education Funding Map is a deliberately-small proof of concept scoped to one sector, built from publicly documented relationships. See the map's own notes for what it can and can't show.
  • Both are self-contained (no external chart libraries) and keyboard/pointer friendly. Got a dataset or a sector worth mapping? Tell us.

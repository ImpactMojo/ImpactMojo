Content Map

Every piece of ImpactMojo, drawn as a constellation of topics. Each dot is a theme; two themes are linked when they show up together across the library. Drag to explore, scroll to zoom, and click any topic to see the content behind it.

Drag · scroll to zoom · click a topic

Click a topic to see the courses, dives, games, and readings tagged with it. Bigger dots are broader themes; dots that sit close together tend to appear together.

How this map is built

  • Built live from the site search index, so it always reflects what's actually published. The map shows the top themes by how much content carries them.
  • A link between two topics means they are tagged on the same items often enough to suggest a real relationship; thicker links = stronger overlap. Positions come from a force layout, not a fixed taxonomy.
  • Colour groups content into families (courses, reading companions, dives, interactive, handouts, dataverse, writing). A topic takes the colour of the family it appears in most.
  • This is a discovery tool, not an exhaustive index: use the filters to focus, and the catalog for the complete list.

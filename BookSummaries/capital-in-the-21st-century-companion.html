Quick Reference — Piketty's machinery at a glance

  • r > g — when the return on capital exceeds the economy's growth rate, wealth concentrates and inherited wealth outpaces earned income (the fundamental force for divergence).
  • First Fundamental Law: α = r × β — capital's share of income equals the return on capital times the capital/income ratio (an accounting identity).
  • Second Fundamental Law: β = s / g — in the long run the capital/income ratio converges to the savings rate over the growth rate; slower growth means a more capital-heavy economy.
  • The U-curve — wealth/income inequality was high in the Belle Époque, collapsed with the two World Wars, the Depression and post-war progressive taxation, and has risen again since the 1970s.
  • Always attribute to Piketty — this is a study companion, not a replacement for the book.

How to use this dictionary

Read an entry

Each entry gives the term, the kind of term and the start of its definition. Open it for the full definition, an example, related terms, sources and the record of how it was written.

Know what you are quoting

Every entry says whether an editor has reviewed it, whether it records a source and whether a language model wrote its first draft.

Study the terms

Study shows one term at a time with the definition on the other side. Mark the terms you know, and they are remembered on this device.

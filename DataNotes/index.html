Data Notes

Independent data investigations — where we take one public dataset, dig in, and show you what it really says.

Each Data Note starts from a number that deserves a second look. We chart it ourselves, make an argument, and — just as importantly — spell out what the data can't tell you. Every source is linked; every chart has a data table behind it. Free, browser-based, no login. New investigations as the data lands. Looking for annotated reading lists instead? Those are our Deep Dives.

Have a dataset worth digging into?

If you know a number — a budget line, a survey result, an administrative figure — that deserves a closer, honest look, pitch us. We build the charts and show our working.

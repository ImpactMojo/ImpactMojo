Simulations

Eighteen games you play in the browser, each built around one idea from economics or development practice. You make the choices and watch the consequences: overfish a shared sea, bid against the winner’s curse, run an epidemic response or split a household’s day between paid and unpaid work. They are also part of the Game Library, which adds 117 crosswords, quizzes and word puzzles.

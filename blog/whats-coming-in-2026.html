What We've Shipped in 2026 (So Far)

The ImpactMojo Team Updated July 11, 2026 5 min read
South Asia capacity building

Last December we laid out a roadmap for 2026 built around three themes: deeper tools for research and evaluation, richer content across more languages and topics, and stronger community through coaching and peer learning.

A roadmap is only worth anything if you come back and mark it honestly. So here, at the halfway point, is exactly that — the same three themes, but with each promise scored: what's live, what's in progress, and what's still ahead. No airbrushing.

The Scorecard

Theme 1 Deeper research tools

Sampling Design Studio Live

The promised Power & Sample Lab shipped — and grew into a full Sampling Design Studio: cluster design, ICC adjustments, attrition buffers and a defensible written plan, tuned for real field conditions. It absorbed the old standalone sample-size calculator.

The wider Labs & Premium Tools shelf Live

Beyond sampling, the Theory of Change and Logframe builders, an Indicator Workbench, and a growing set of premium tools — ToR builder, RQ builder, qualitative-insights lab, viz cookbook and more — are all live in the Premium Tools shelf.

Survey Cost Estimator & Field Timeline Planner Still ahead

The INR-benchmarked budgeting tool and the monsoon-and-festival-aware scheduler haven't shipped yet. They're still on the plan, not abandoned.

Theme 2 Richer content & languages

Flagship courses, levelled up Live

All 17 flagship courses now open every module with a concept diagram and a real open-access key reading, and each ships a printable Course Notes PDF you can own and annotate offline. The foundational 101 library grew to 53 self-paced decks, adding GenAI for Practitioners, the DPDP Act, Safeguarding & PSEA and Disability Inclusion.

Regional languages In progress

Site-wide translation into Hindi, Bengali, Tamil, Telugu and Marathi is live via the language switcher on every page. Fully native course instruction in each language — rather than translation of the English — is the deeper goal still underway.

Deep Dives & a Case Study Library In progress

The Deep Dives library — annotated, sourced reading paths on real development questions — is live and growing. A permissioned library of full evaluation case studies from South Asian organisations is the piece still ahead.

Theme 3 Stronger community & credentials

Verifiable credentials Live

The big one shipped. Four Assessed Tracks (MEL, Data & Technology, Policy & Economics, and AI for M&E) now pair free study with a capstone review and a verifiable certificate, and a Credential Upgrade lets free-pathway completers add just the assessment without re-buying content.

Coaching & Dojos Live

The community layer is open: 1:1 Coaching for practitioners who want guided support, and Dojos for hands-on, practice-based sessions.

Structured peer cohorts Still ahead

Small groups moving through a course together, with structured discussion and accountability, is the community piece we still owe you.

What we shipped that wasn't on the list

Some of the most useful work this year wasn't in the December plan at all — it came out of watching how people actually move around the platform:

The Libraries hub Live

One home at /libraries for every collection — courses, labs, games, reading companions, deep dives, the Dataverse and more.

A searchable Site Map Live

Every destination on the platform in one filterable, theme-aware page — type to jump anywhere.

Course Notes PDFs Live

A print-ready notes booklet for every flagship — diagrams, worked examples and key readings to work through offline.

A two-round content refresh Live

A page-by-page audit brought the headline figures across the deep dives and 101 decks up to their latest published sources.

Built With You

Almost everything above traces back to a conversation with a practitioner. The Sampling Design Studio grew out of M&E managers telling us they spend hours on calculations that should take minutes. The credential work reflects people who'd done the learning and needed a way to prove it. The Libraries hub exists because the platform had quietly become a behemoth and people asked for a single door in.

How You Can Shape What's Next

  • Use the labs: Your usage patterns tell us what works and what needs improvement
  • Send feedback: The "Feedback" button isn't decoration—we read everything
  • Join beta testing: Early access to new tools in exchange for honest input
  • Share your challenges: The problems you face become the features we build

Staying Accessible

As we grow, we're committed to keeping our core content free. The courses, the labs, the games—these remain open to anyone who wants to learn.

Premium features like advanced coaching, certification, and organisational tools help sustain the platform. But the educational mission comes first. A programme officer in rural Odisha and a consultant in Singapore should both be able to build the same foundational skills.

"Quality development education shouldn't be a luxury good available only to those who can attend expensive workshops in metropolitan cities."

The Bigger Picture

Our ultimate goal isn't to build a platform—it's to build capacity across the sector. Every practitioner who strengthens their skills improves the programmes they work on, which benefits the communities those programmes serve.

If ImpactMojo helps even a fraction of the development workforce do their work more effectively, that's a multiplier effect worth pursuing.

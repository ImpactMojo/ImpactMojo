Why Practice Packs?

Most practitioner training in India sits at two extremes: a one-hour workshop too thin to produce anything, or a multi-week course asking commitment most practitioners can't make. Practice Packs sit in the middle: focused enough to finish in a working day, deep enough to leave you with something usable Monday morning.

  • Subject Packs: per-domain. The first instinct most clients have is "we need a livelihoods evaluation" or "an SEL evaluation," not "a survey instrument." Subject packs meet that instinct.
  • Method Packs: cross-cutting. Once you've done two or three Subject Packs, you'll want to deepen specific skills. Method packs go deep on one skill.
  • Lab-like interactivity: every pack is now a working tool, not just reading material. Inputs auto-save, capstone builds itself, export when done.
  • Free preview, no login: the first two modules of every pack are open to all. Your inputs stay in your browser; nothing transmitted. Unlock the full pack with Premium or a one-time ₹299 purchase.
  • Living documents: updated as practice evolves and reader feedback arrives.

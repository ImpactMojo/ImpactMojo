The question bank

The bank has 500 original multiple-choice questions on policy and economics for development, in ten sections of 50. Each question has four options and one correct answer. The file includes a short answer key and a second key that explains each answer in one sentence, which you can read out after a test or hand to learners.

The questions range from recall to application and analysis. They were written for this bank and checked for accuracy.

Sections: Microeconomics foundations, Macroeconomics & development, Welfare economics & market failures, Poverty & inequality measurement, Development economics, Public finance & taxation, The policy process & analysis, Behavioural economics & policy, Political economy & institutions, Labour, trade & industrial policy.

How trainers use it

  • Pre and post tests. Draw two parallel sets of questions from the same sections. Give one before a module and the other after it, so a learner cannot score higher on the second simply by remembering the first.
  • Module quizzes. Each section of 50 lines up with a topic, so a short quiz at the end of a session can come from the section that session covered.
  • Item analysis. After a test, count how many learners chose each option. A question nearly everyone gets right is too easy to tell learners apart, and a wrong option nobody picks is doing no work. Both are worth replacing from the bank.
  • Discussion. The explanation key gives the reasoning behind each answer, which makes a wrong answer a starting point for a class discussion.

See trouble before it arrives

Evaluations run into the same trouble again and again: a facility list that arrives late, a monsoon, a reviewer who changes the questions in week ten. Most of it can be seen in advance, and a log makes the team say it aloud, give it an owner and a date, and look at it every week.

This workbook has four sheets, one each for risks, assumptions, issues and dependencies. Risks are scored by chance and effect from 1 to 3, and the score sets a band: Act now, Watch or Accept. A Word guide gives a five-step method, a list of typical evaluation risks and a worked example.

It works beside the Weekly Status Note, which reports the log to the client, and the After-Action Review, which turns closed items into lessons.

FAQ

What format is it?

An Excel workbook (.xlsx) with live formulas and dropdowns, plus a Word guide. It also opens in Google Sheets and LibreOffice.

How is the score worked out?

The chance (1 to 3) is multiplied by the effect (1 to 3). A score of 6 or more is Act now, 3 or 4 is Watch and 1 or 2 is Accept.

Can I use it for more than one assignment?

Yes. Save a copy for each assignment so that each log carries its own history.

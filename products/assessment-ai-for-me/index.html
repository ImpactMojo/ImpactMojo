This kit is small. It is the question bank and one set of course notes, because ImpactMojo has no trainer deck, workbook, refresher or poster on AI for M&E yet.

The question bank

The bank has 500 original multiple-choice questions on ai for monitoring and evaluation, in 13 sections of 50. Each question has four options and one correct answer. The file includes a short answer key and a second key that explains each answer in one sentence, which you can read out after a test or hand to learners.

The questions range from recall to application and analysis. They were written for this bank and checked for accuracy.

Sections: AI & ML foundations for evaluators, Large language models & generative AI, AI for qualitative analysis, AI for quantitative & data tasks, Prompt engineering for M&E workflows, AI-assisted reporting & evidence synthesis, Bias, fairness & validity in AI for M&E, AI ethics, privacy & responsible use, AI tools & the evaluation workflow, AI limitations, risks & verification.

How trainers use it

  • Pre and post tests. Draw two parallel sets of questions from the same sections. Give one before a module and the other after it, so a learner cannot score higher on the second simply by remembering the first.
  • Module quizzes. Each section of 50 lines up with a topic, so a short quiz at the end of a session can come from the section that session covered.
  • Item analysis. After a test, count how many learners chose each option. A question nearly everyone gets right is too easy to tell learners apart, and a wrong option nobody picks is doing no work. Both are worth replacing from the bank.
  • Discussion. The explanation key gives the reasoning behind each answer, which makes a wrong answer a starting point for a class discussion.

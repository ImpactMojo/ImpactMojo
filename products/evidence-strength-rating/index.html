Make the strength of each finding visible

A report in which every finding is written in the same confident voice hides the difference between what the evaluation showed and what it suggests. A reader cannot tell a result from a survey of 1,200 women from an impression gathered in four interviews.

This guide gives you five tests, each scored 0, 1 or 2: sources, method fit, consistency, bias risk and coverage. One cap stops a strong total from hiding a weak core. A rating sheet records the reasons, and a table gives the words to use in the report for each rating. A worked example rates six findings from an evaluation, and shows one capped at Moderate and why.

The inception report carries a short evidence scale for planning. This guide is for rating the findings once the data are in. It pairs with the Inception Report & Evaluation Matrix Template and leads into the Findings Storyline Template.

What's inside

  • Five tests, each scored 0, 1 or 2, with a description of every score
  • A cap that stops a high total hiding a flawed method or a high bias risk
  • A rating sheet for each finding and a second-reader step
  • Report wording for strong, moderate and limited findings
  • Five ways a rating drifts upward, and examples of the method-fit test
  • A worked example that rates six findings, one of them capped

FAQ

What format is it?

An editable DOCX file that opens in Microsoft Office, Google, LibreOffice and Pages.

Is this a standard scale?

No. It is a simple method in the spirit of the grading used in systematic reviews, built so that an evaluation team can use it in an afternoon and a second reader can repeat it.

Can I add tests or change the cut-offs?

Yes. Write your choices into the inception report and apply them to every finding.

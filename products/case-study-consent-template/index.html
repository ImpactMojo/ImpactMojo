Consent for the interview is not consent for the story

A case study is the part of an evaluation most likely to be read, shared and quoted, and the part most likely to harm someone named in it. A person who agrees to answer questions for a report may be unwilling to be the face of a success story, and may regret it when the story travels.

This pack gives you the case study in eight sections and the consent process in six steps. A consent form offers four choices of how the person appears and a right to read the draft and to withdraw. A checklist helps you anonymise, and a record sheet lets you find the consent a year later. A box of checked law covers consent under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, children, and the rule against publishing anything that identifies a victim of certain sexual offences.

It builds on the Data Management & Consent Pack and the Ethics and DPDP checklist, and it holds the consent you promised in the Engagement Charter.

What's inside

  • A case study in eight sections, with a rule to state whether the case is typical
  • A consent form for the case study with four choices of how the person appears, and a place for a guardian's consent
  • Law as checked on 21 October 2026: consent, children and the protection of victims' identity
  • An anonymisation checklist and wording for the consent conversation
  • A record sheet that tells you where the consent is and when to check it again
  • A worked example: a health facility case study and its consent record

Why these 4 together

The quality of an evaluation is mostly set before the evaluator is hired. A terms of reference with a vague purpose gets bids that guess at it, and a panel with no shared scoring sheet picks the bid that was best presented.

The ToR template sets out the study. The scoring rubric and the review checklist let a panel compare bids on the same criteria. The causal designs refresher helps a reader without an evaluation background judge whether the design a bidder proposes can answer the question asked.

Why these 4 together

A donor reads a proposal for three things. They want to know what will change and why you think it will, how you will know it changed, and what it will cost. The theory of change canvas answers the first, the logframe the second and the budget the third.

The stakeholder map comes with them because most proposal formats now ask who else is involved and how you will work with them. Filling it in early also shows you which partner letters to request.

Why these 6 together

Most problems in a data-collection round are visible before the first interview. A question that asks two things at once, a consent form that does not say how long data will be kept, or an enumerator team with no plan for a failed tablet will all cost more to fix in the field than at the desk.

This bundle covers the instruments for a survey and for focus groups, the consent and data management forms the DPDP Act 2023 now requires, and two one-page checklists to run through before each round. The sampling refresher is there for the sample size conversation that always comes up.

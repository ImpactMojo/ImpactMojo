Put a course in your LMS

Every ImpactMojo course can be downloaded as a package your institution's learning system can import. Free, no login, and once imported it runs without reaching the internet.

Build a package

The package is built in your browser from the live course, so it is always the current version.

Importing it

Moodle

Turn editing on → Add an activity or resource → SCORM package → upload the .zip. For Common Cartridge use Import instead.

Canvas

Settings → Import Course Content → choose SCORM package or Common Cartridge → upload.

Blackboard

Build Content → Content Package (SCORM) → upload the .zip.

Google Classroom

Classroom does not read SCORM. Use the Single HTML file, upload it to Drive, and post it as material.

A package is a snapshot of the course on the day you download it. Courses get revised, and law and statistics in them go out of date — so if you are teaching from an export made a while ago, download it again before the term starts.

What you may do with it

Everything is CC BY-NC-ND 4.0. Use it in your teaching with a credit line — “Content from ImpactMojo — impactmojo.in”. You can charge for your facilitation; you cannot sell the materials or publish modified versions. For adaptations or translations, write to us — we are flexible with mission-aligned use.

The export deliberately leaves out our analytics, sign-in and translation code, so nothing in the package reports back to us and nothing tracks your students except your own LMS.

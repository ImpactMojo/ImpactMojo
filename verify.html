How verification works

Every ImpactMojo certificate carries a unique ID. When you submit one here, we check it directly against the issuing record — no account or sign-in required.

  1. The candidate shares their certificate ID or verification link from their CV, email, or LinkedIn profile.
  2. You paste it above and select Verify.
  3. If it's genuine, you'll see a green Valid card with the holder's name, the course, and the issue date. If not, you'll see a clear Not Found message.
Open Badges 3.0 verifiable credentials. ImpactMojo certificates are also issued as W3C Open Badges 3.0 — portable, cryptographically describable digital credentials. The full machine-readable badge (JSON-LD) and a LinkedIn share link are available on each certificate's detail page, so the credential stays verifiable wherever it travels.

One number, and how we got it

Each card here takes one figure from India's public data and sets it beside another. Every figure is computed from the sources listed under it, and the table shows the working for every state, so you can check it or use it.

CSR, 2022-23

Bihar received ₹19 of company CSR spending per resident in 2022-23. Maharashtra received ₹436.

Bihar's multidimensional poverty index is 4.8 times Maharashtra's. Maharashtra's CSR per resident is 23 times Bihar's.

How it is worked out

  • CSR per resident is a state's 2022-23 CSR spending divided by its projected population on 1 October 2022, the middle of the financial year.
  • States are ranked by NITI Aayog's multidimensional poverty index (MPI) for 2019-21, and where two share an MPI at the three decimals NITI prints, by the share of people who are poor (the headcount ratio). That decides Himachal Pradesh and Punjab.
  • The five-state figures pool spending and population across the five, so a small state does not count as much as a large one.
  • The ₹7,030 crore recorded as "PAN India", "PAN India (Other Centralized Funds)" or "NEC/Not Mentioned" is not assigned to any state and is left out. Spreading it by population would raise every state by the same amount per resident and change none of the ranks.
  • The CSR Explorer divides by the 2011 census, the last count taken, so its per-resident figures run higher than these for fast-growing states.
  • The ministry records the state the company reported for each project. A company headquartered in Mumbai can report national work as Maharashtra's, so part of the gap measures where companies are based.

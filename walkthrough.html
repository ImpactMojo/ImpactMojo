What ImpactMojo is
A free library for people who do development work
ImpactMojo is a free educational platform for development professionals, NGOs, researchers and impact practitioners across South Asia. The courses, tools and readings are written by practitioners, with South Asian cases and Indian data.
  • The core library is free and needs no login.
  • Paid plans and products build on top and never lock the basics away.
  • Content is released under CC BY-NC-ND 4.0.
Who it is for. Programme staff, MEL teams, researchers, students, teachers and the organisations that train them.

Features
What works the same way everywhere
Search
Press Ctrl+K or Cmd+K, or the search button, to search courses, tools, readings and data at once.
Reading settings
Light and dark themes, and an OpenDyslexic reading font, from the settings button in the header.
Translation
Pages can be switched into हिन्दी, தமிழ், বাংলা, मराठी, తెలుగు from the language switch.
Accessibility
Core pages target WCAG 2.1 AA, and an automated audit runs over every page each day. Read the accessibility statement

Features
An account is optional, and adds a few things
  • Bookmarks, notes and progress tracking across courses.
  • Free certificates on completion, which anyone can check on Verify a certificate.
  • Offline reading: pages you have visited open without a connection, and a flagship course can be downloaded.
  • Nothing in the free library requires an account. Create one only if you want these.

Teaching
Three ways instructors use the material
Semester companion
A flagship course as the backbone, one module a week, with its self-check as a formative test and a Studio output as coursework.
Workshop kit
Open with a game, teach from a 101 deck, then run a Studio as the hands-on block. No accounts needed.
Team cohort
A team takes a flagship together and finishes on one of 15 live case challenges.
Teach with ImpactMojo maps the courses to syllabi.

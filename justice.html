Who is actually in prison?

Three in every four people held in an Indian prison have not been convicted of anything. They are waiting for a trial, in a system holding 45 million pending cases. This traces who is inside, how long they have been there, and what is happening in the courts that hold them.

A right that mostly went unused

Section 436A of the Code of Criminal Procedure: a person who has spent half the maximum sentence for the offence charged in custody, without trial, shall be released on bond.

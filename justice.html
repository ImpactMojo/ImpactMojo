Who is actually in prison?

Nearly three in every four people held in an Indian prison have not been convicted of anything. They are waiting for a trial, in district courts holding nearly 50 million pending cases. This traces who is inside, how long they have been there, and what is happening in the courts that hold them.

A right that reaches very few

Section 479 of the Bharatiya Nagarik Suraksha Sanhita, which replaced Section 436A of the Code of Criminal Procedure on 1 July 2024: a person who has spent half the maximum sentence for the offence charged in custody, without trial, shall be released by the court on bail. A first-time offender is to be released on bond after one third.

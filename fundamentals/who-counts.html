Fundamentals

What India does not measure,
and what that costs

India runs some of the largest household surveys in the world. This page is about the other side of them. Eight things the country does not count, counts far below what independent estimates find, or counted once and has not counted since.

Each gap opens the evidence that it exists, what follows from it, and the closest substitute anyone has.

How to read it

Three ways a population goes missing

Not every gap is the same kind of gap, and the remedy differs. One needs a question added to an instrument. One needs the instrument believed. One needs the survey run again.

Never askedNo national instrument collects it. There is nothing to correct, only something to add.
Counted, badlyAn official figure exists and sits far below what independent estimates find.
FrozenIt was counted once. The number still circulates and the year keeps receding.

Why this page exists

Building the Wheel of Power meant sourcing 113 statistics, and the same holes kept appearing. Some axes had rich data and some had none, and the pattern of which was which was not random.

An absence is harder to cite than a presence, so the rule for this page is the same as the rest of the series. Every claim that something is not measured names the instrument checked and the year it was checked.

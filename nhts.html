How India gets to work and school

In October 2026 the National Statistics Office released the first all-India survey of household travel. Between July 2025 and June 2026 its enumerators visited 14,274 villages and 10,377 urban blocks and asked 4,92,023 households, covering 20,34,371 people, how they travel to work, to school or college, on errands and on journeys that keep them away overnight. Only trips of a kilometre or more were recorded. This page draws every all-India figure the press note publishes.

How workers get to work

The main way of travelling, among workers aged 15 and above who make a trip of a kilometre or more to a workplace, fixed or not. "Main" means the mode used for the longest part of the trip.

Who makes the trip at all

The share of workers, or of students, who travel a kilometre or more to work or to study on a usual day. Someone who works at home, or whose school is round the corner, is in the denominator and not in the count.

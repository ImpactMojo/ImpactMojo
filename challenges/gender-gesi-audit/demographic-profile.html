The gap the headline number hides. Across the target area, PVTG communities are 18% of the population but only 3% of participants: a six-fold under-representation that is worst in Narayanpur and Dantewada, the blocks with the largest PVTG populations. Persons with disabilities are an estimated 2.2% of the working-age population but 0.2% of participants. The programme reports a 72% women's participation rate, but holds no data on caste/tribe, disability, marital status or female-headship in its own participant database, so these gaps are invisible in routine reporting.

3. Definitions & sources

  • Population & composition: Census 2011 figures projected to 2024 and cross-checked against the state tribal-welfare department's block profiles.
  • PVTG share: estimated share of population belonging to a notified Particularly Vulnerable Tribal Group (Abujhmaria, Hill Maria, Kamar); a subset of the Scheduled Tribe total.
  • Female-headed households: households reporting an adult woman as the primary decision-maker / head, from the programme's 2023 enrolment-baseline survey.
  • Disability prevalence: estimated working-age prevalence, triangulated from Census 2011 disability data and NFHS-5; treated as a planning estimate, not a register.
  • Participant figures: Grameen Shakti participant database, midpoint extract (4,180 enrolments). PVTG and disability shares were reconstructed by the mid-term review team, as the database does not record these fields.

4. Data-quality notes

  • The participant database records sex but not caste/tribe, disability, marital status or female-headship; the participant-level PVTG and disability shares here were estimated by hand during the mid-term review and should be read as indicative.
  • Population composition relies on projected 2011 Census shares; actual 2024 shares may differ slightly, but the direction and scale of the participation gaps are robust.
  • Disability prevalence is widely under-counted in Census and survey data, so the true participation gap for persons with disabilities is likely larger than shown.

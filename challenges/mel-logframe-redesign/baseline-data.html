Definitions & sources

  • Neonatal mortality rate: modelled from HMIS facility deaths and NFHS-5 district estimates; community deaths are under-reported, especially in remote blocks of Sheopur.
  • Institutional delivery: share of registered deliveries occurring in a public or private health facility (HMIS, 2023–24).
  • ANC 4+ coverage: share of pregnant women completing at least four antenatal check-ups (HMIS / programme registers).
  • Activity counts (camps, kits, counselling): HealthBridge programme registers for the first project year.

Data-quality notes

  • No dedicated baseline survey was conducted; figures triangulate HMIS, NFHS-5 and a first-year rapid review.
  • Neonatal-death counts are the weakest series: denominators (live births) are modelled, and first-week deaths are likely undercounted.
  • Activity counts are reliable but capture volume, not quality or reach relative to need.

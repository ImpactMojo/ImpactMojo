Twelve printers are registered on the departmental panel. Across the six sessions above, the three firms named won 41 of 51 lot-awards, amounting to roughly 80% of contract value. L1 margins between the top three bids averaged 0.4–1.1% of estimated cost. No penalty for late delivery has been recovered in any of the six years; liquidated-damages notices were issued in 2021–22 and 2025–26 and subsequently waived or left unadjudicated. No audit para in this period concerns delivery timing or classroom availability of books.

The Manual prescribes no calendar working backwards from 1 April. Each office's duty is defined by the file it receives, not by the school-opening date. The only performance record maintained on individual officials is the audit-objection register.

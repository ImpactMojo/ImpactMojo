District Nutrition Indicators: 2024–25

12 districts · four quarters (Q1–Q4) · the data behind the dashboard you are critiquing

What this is. This is the raw indicator data that feeds the current dashboard (rendered). The dashboard charts are built from exactly these numbers, so when you redesign it, you can re-plot the same series. Note that the dataset itself carries the inconsistent formatting that the live dashboard inherits.
↧ Download raw data (CSV)

Indicators in this dataset

  • Stunting %: share of enrolled children under 5 with low height-for-age (state survey, modelled to district level).
  • Wasting rate: share of children under 5 with low weight-for-height (recorded in the source file as a percentage string, e.g. 17%).
  • Anganwadi coverage: operational anganwadi centres as a share of sanctioned centres (recorded as a decimal, e.g. 0.91, except Q4 which someone entered as 94%).
  • THR distribution: Take-Home Ration packets distributed to eligible beneficiaries in the quarter (raw count).
  • Growth-monitoring %: share of enrolled children weighed at least once in the quarter.
  • Children enrolled: children registered at anganwadi centres (raw count, but Q3 rows were entered as 1.56 lakh “to save space”).

The full file has 48 rows (12 districts × 4 quarters) and 8 columns. Districts span the state: from better-performing Kota and Sikar to the high-burden tribal southern belt (Banswara, Dungarpur, Udaipur) and the arid west (Barmer, Bikaner). The formatting is reproduced exactly as it appears in the source register.

Known data-entry issues (reproduced, not fixed)

  • Mixed types within a column: anganwadi_coverage mixes decimals (0.91) and percent strings (94%); children_enrolled mixes raw counts and “lakh” text.
  • Wasting stored as text: the % sign is part of the value, so it will not sort or average numerically without cleaning.
  • Trailing-zero inconsistency (37.40 vs 37.9, 76.10 vs 74) the same precision is not used across rows.
  • These are the kinds of issues a redesign has to resolve before charting, not after.

Score Distribution by Demographic Dimension

Vulnerability Targeting Model v2.1 · 47-household audit sample · scoring run: Q3 2024

How to use this document. These cross-tabs summarise the anonymized model outputs (Document 1) along the dimensions named in the case. Figures are reported without interpretation; reading the disparities and tracing their causes: with reference to the feature importance rankings (Document 3): is the substance of your audit.

All means are computed over the 47-household audit sample. Mean score is the average predicted vulnerability score (0–100). Eligible % is the share of households in the group flagged for the priority list (score ≥ 55). Mean completeness is the average data_completeness_pct for the group.

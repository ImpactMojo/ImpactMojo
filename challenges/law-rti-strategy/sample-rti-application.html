Sample RTI Application Format

A reusable template, a worked PMGSY example, and a first-appeal format

Templates for study, not legal advice. These formats illustrate how a well-drafted RTI application and first appeal are structured. Application/copying fees, the mode of fee payment, and the exact appellate authority are fixed by the Central or your State (here, Jharkhand) RTI Rules: check them before filing. The aim is to teach precise, itemised drafting, not to substitute for the actual rules.
How to use this document. This is one of three case files for the challenge “Draft an RTI Strategy for an Accountability Campaign”. Read it with the RTI Act key sections and the PMGSY guidelines summary. Use the blank template for your own drafts and adapt the worked example for Jan Sarokar's submission.

1. What makes a strong RTI application

  • One subject, specific questions. Number each question; ask for identifiable documents (“certified copy of the Measurement Book for Work No. ___”) rather than vague explanations.
  • Define the period. Always state the date range so the PIO cannot claim ambiguity.
  • Ask for records, not opinions. The Act gives access to existing records; phrase requests as “provide a certified copy of…” / “permit inspection of…”.
  • Right to inspect. For physical works, invoke s.2(j) and ask to inspect the road / records and take certified copies / samples.
  • No reasons. You need not justify why you want it (s.6(2)); do not volunteer motive.
  • Fee & BPL. Enclose the prescribed application fee; if the applicant is Below Poverty Line, state it and attach proof: no fee is payable (s.7(5)).

2. Blank application template

To, The Public Information Officer, [Name of office, e.g. Programme Implementation Unit, PMGSY, ___ Block] [Full address] Subject: Application for information under the Right to Information Act, 2005 Sir / Madam, Under Section 6(1) of the Right to Information Act, 2005, I request the following information for the period [from DD-MM-YYYY to DD-MM-YYYY]: 1. [Specific, itemised request: name the document] 2. [Specific, itemised request] 3. [Specific, itemised request] I wish to receive the information as [certified photocopies / inspection of records / inspection of works under s.2(j) / electronic form]. The prescribed application fee of [amount] is enclosed by [IPO / DD / court-fee stamp / as per applicable RTI Rules]. [OR] I am a person Below the Poverty Line; a copy of my BPL card / certificate is enclosed, and no fee is payable under the proviso to Section 7(5). If any part of this information is held by another public authority, kindly transfer the relevant part under Section 6(3) and inform me. If any part is refused, kindly provide reasons, the period within which an appeal may be preferred, and the particulars of the First Appellate Authority (Section 7(8)), and provide all non-exempt portions under Section 10. Yours faithfully, [Signature] Name: [Full name] Address: [Full postal address] Phone / Email: [contact] Date: [DD-MM-YYYY] Place: [place]

3. Worked example: PMGSY road irregularities

This is a Phase-1 application for Jan Sarokar, targeting the sanction and completion records for the disputed roads. (A full strategy would file parallel/follow-up applications for quality reports, muster rolls and tender records: see the note after the example.)

To, The Public Information Officer, Programme Implementation Unit (PMGSY), [___] Block, [District], Jharkhand. Subject: Application under the RTI Act, 2005, records of PMGSY roads sanctioned in [___] Gram Panchayat / Block Sir / Madam, Under Section 6(1) of the Right to Information Act, 2005, I request the following information for the period 01-04-[YYYY] to [date of this application]: 1. A list of all PMGSY road works sanctioned in [___] Block / Gram Panchayat during the above period, with for each work: the work name and sanction/package number, sanctioned length (km), sanctioned cost, name of the contractor, and date of the completion certificate (if issued). 2. Certified copies of the Detailed Project Report / detailed estimate and the technical sanction for the following works: [list work numbers, or "Work No. ___"]. 3. Certified copies of the completion certificate and the final bill paid for each of the works listed at point 2, showing the final measured quantities and the total amount paid to the contractor. 4. The total expenditure released and paid, work-wise, for the above works, with copies of the running and final bills. 5. Permission to inspect, under Section 2(j) of the Act, the said road works on the ground and the related records (Measurement Books and bills), and to take notes and certified copies; kindly intimate a date and time for inspection. I wish to receive items 1-4 as certified photocopies. No reason for seeking this information is required under Section 6(2). The prescribed application fee of [amount per applicable Jharkhand RTI Rules] is enclosed. [OR, if applicable] The applicant is Below the Poverty Line (BPL card no. [___], copy enclosed); no fee is payable under the proviso to Section 7(5). If any record is more closely connected with another public authority (e.g. the SRRDA or the State Rural Works Department), kindly transfer that part under Section 6(3) and inform me. Should any portion be claimed as exempt, kindly note that these are records of expenditure of public funds and that the larger public interest in disclosure outweighs any claimed harm (Section 8(2)); provide all non-exempt portions under Section 10; and, where Section 11 applies to the contractor's records, follow the third-party procedure and decide in the public interest. Yours faithfully, [Signature] Name: [Applicant, on behalf of Jan Sarokar] Address / Phone / Email: [contact] Date: [DD-MM-YYYY] Place: [place]
Phasing note. Keep applications focused. Phase 1 (above) establishes what was sanctioned and paid. Phase 2 then targets quality (Tier-1 quality-control test records, SQM and NQM inspection reports and grades, action taken on adverse grades) and labour (muster rolls and wage-payment records, to test the ghost-worker allegation). Phase 3 targets the tender (NIT, bidder list, comparative statement, award note and any conflict-of-interest declarations) to test the contractor/BDO relationship. Splitting topics keeps each request answerable and makes a refusal of any one topic easy to appeal.

4. First-appeal template (RTI s.19(1))

Use this if you get no reply within 30 days (deemed refusal), a refusal, an improper exemption claim, or an excessive fee. File within 30 days with the officer senior in rank to the PIO.

To, The First Appellate Authority, [designation of officer senior to the PIO, e.g. Executive Engineer / SE, PMGSY] [Office & address] Subject: First Appeal under Section 19(1) of the RTI Act, 2005 Sir / Madam, 1. I filed an RTI application dated [date] (copy enclosed) with the PIO, [office], with fee, seeking [one-line summary]. 2. The status is: [no reply received within the 30-day limit and the request stands deemed refused under s.7(2)] / [the PIO replied on (date) (copy enclosed) and refused / partially refused on the ground that ___]. 3. This appeal is preferred on the following grounds: (a) [Deemed refusal: the statutory time limit under s.7 has expired without a decision.] (b) [The exemption invoked (e.g. s.8(1)(d) commercial confidence) does not apply to records of public expenditure, and the public-interest override under s.8(2) requires disclosure.] (c) [Non-exempt portions were not severed and provided as required by s.10.] (d) [The fee demanded is excessive / information was due free of charge under s.7(6) because of the delay.] 4. The burden of proving that the refusal was justified lies on the PIO under Section 19(5). I therefore request that the PIO be directed to provide the information sought, free of charge in view of the delay, and that the appeal be allowed. Yours faithfully, [Signature] Name / Address / Contact: [___] Date: [DD-MM-YYYY] Place: [place] Enclosures: copy of RTI application + fee receipt; copy of PIO's reply (if any).
If the first appeal fails. File a second appeal within 90 days to the Jharkhand State Information Commission (s.19(3)), enclosing the application, the PIO's reply, the first appeal and its order. Ask the Commission to direct disclosure and to consider a penalty on the PIO under Section 20 (up to ₹25,000) for any malafide or unjustified denial.

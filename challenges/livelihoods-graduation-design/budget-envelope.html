Saksham Bharat Philanthropy · Grant Terms Annex B

Budget Envelope: Unnati Graduation Pilot

Udyam Sakhi Sansthan · 400 ultra-poor households · Katoria & Chandan blocks, Banka, Bihar

Total sanction: ₹3.20 crore over 24 months · Disbursement: 6-monthly tranches against milestones · Status: caps are firm; line allocation is yours to propose
How to use this document. The funder has fixed the ceiling, four cost caps and an M&E floor: everything else is for you to allocate. Your submission must include a per-household cost build-up that fits inside this envelope, grounded in the prices in Document 1: District Livelihood Profile.

1. The envelope

Total grant is ₹3,20,00,000 (₹3.2 crore) over 24 months for 400 households: an all-inclusive ₹80,000 per household covering transfers, delivery, staff, training, M&E and overheads. No cost extension will be considered; an under-spend does not roll over.

2. Fixed caps and floors

Term Cap / floor Implied maximum (400 HH) Funder rationale
Productive asset transfer ≤ ₹18,000 per household (cash or in kind, one or more tranches) ₹72,00,000 In line with NRLM-era graduation pilots; larger transfers distort local asset prices
Consumption stipend ≤ ₹1,000 per household per month, for a maximum of 7 months ₹28,00,000 Smooth consumption during asset gestation only; not an income substitute. You choose which months and which households
Coaching staff Ratio no thinner than 1 coach per 50 households, at ₹18,000/month per coach (fully loaded) ₹34,56,000 (8 coaches × 24 months) Weekly-touch coaching is the graduation model's engine; do not economise here
Monitoring, evaluation & learning Floor, not cap: ≥ 5% of total grant ₹16,00,000 minimum Must fund baseline, graduation-criteria tracking, and an endline someone else could believe

The four fixed lines above account for ₹1.51 crore at maximum utilisation. The remaining ~₹1.69 crore is unallocated: technical training, veterinary and input support, pashu sakhi honoraria, SHG formation in uncovered hamlets, transport, supervision, convergence liaison and organisational overheads (overheads capped at 10% of total) must all come from it. Show your working.

3. Benchmarks the funder will hold you against

Programme Reported cost per household Note
Bandhan THP (West Bengal, RCT) ~₹35,000–40,000 (2010s prices) Benchmark-cited Indian graduation RCT; large, persistent effects on consumption, assets and income at 3 and 7 years
Six-country graduation RCTs (Banerjee et al. 2015) US$358–US$1,455 PPP Positive benefit–cost ratios in 5 of 6 sites; effects driven by the full sequenced package, not any single component
Satat Jeevikoparjan Yojana (Bihar govt / JEEViKA) ~₹1,00,000 all-in The state's own at-scale adaptation, running in Banka; your design should complement it, not duplicate it
Your envelope
Unnati pilot (this grant) ₹80,000 all-in Mid-range by these benchmarks. The funder expects the evidence-backed sequence intact: if you drop or thin a component, justify it against the RCT evidence

4. Convergence expectations

  • Government entitlements are not to be paid from this grant: MGNREGA wages, social-security pensions, PDS, Ujjwala and PMAY must be pursued through convergence. Budget only the facilitation cost.
  • SHG formation and bank linkage should route through JEEViKA (NRLM); the grant may fund community mobilisers to seed SHGs in the ~40% of hamlets without one, subject to a written understanding with the JEEViKA block team.
  • Households already receiving Satat Jeevikoparjan Yojana support are ineligible; your targeting step must screen for this.

5. The funder’s two hard questions

From the grant committee's minute, quoted verbatim. Your submission must answer both directly.

Q1. What happens in month 25? “Every line in this budget stops at month 24. Show us, household by household in your model, what keeps incomes rising when the coach stops visiting, and which institutions (SHG, VO, pashu sakhi network, the aggregator, government schemes) will still be standing.”
Q2. How will you know someone has graduated, not just received? “A household that ate the stipend, sold the goats and rejoined the kiln queue has received. Define graduation criteria we can verify from your MIS: measurable, time-bound, and not gameable by the field team whose performance is judged on them.”

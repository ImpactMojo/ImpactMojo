District education support · Khunti district, Jharkhand · Blocks: Khunti, Karra, Torpa, Murhu, Arki

Extracted: 30 Jun 2026 · Sample rows shown; full sheets run to thousands of rows · Compiled by: District data operator
These extracts are shown exactly as the sheets are kept today. Nothing has been cleaned. The inconsistencies you see in these samples occur throughout the full files. Your job is to design the data model and transform plan that turns them into a defensible dashboard: read the Director’s brief before you start.

Buniyaad Programme: Director’s Dashboard Brief

District education support · Khunti district, Jharkhand

Ref: SSF/KHU/2026-27/041 · Circulated: 26 Jun 2026 · Response requested within two weeks
This memo is your terms of reference. Everything you propose in your submission (data model, transform plan, measures and layout) should be traceable to a question or constraint stated here. Read it alongside the data landscape document.
To Data & MIS consultant (dashboard assignment)
From Dr. Meera Xalxo, Director, Shiksha Setu Foundation
Cc District Programme Lead, Khunti; Data Operator
Date 26 June 2026
Subject One dashboard for Buniyaad: requirements and non-negotiables

Next quarter I will sit across the table from the District Education Officer, our board, and possibly our funder’s auditors, and all three will ask versions of the same question: is this programme working? Right now the honest answer is “we have three spreadsheets and a headache.” I want one dashboard. Before you build anything in Power BI, I want a written specification I can read and challenge.

The questions the dashboard must answer

  1. Are children learning? What share of assessed children are at grade level in Hindi and Maths, and is that share moving between Term 1 and Term 2? I want this by class and by block.
  2. Which blocks are lagging? Not just the average: show me where Arki or Murhu sits relative to the district, in learning and in coverage, on the same footing.
  3. Are our visits reaching the weakest schools? How many distinct schools did we actually visit each month, what share of the 214 have never been visited, and are visits concentrating on the easy-to-reach schools near the block headquarters?
  4. Are girls and SC/ST children progressing equally? Every learning number above should be splittable by gender and social category, and I want at least one view that puts the equity gap itself on screen, not buried in a filter.

Constraints you must design within

  • Free Power BI tier only. We have Power BI Desktop and free-tier publishing. No Premium capacity, no paid gateways, no per-user Pro licences for the field team. If your design assumes features we cannot afford, it is the wrong design.
  • Monthly refresh, done by one person. Prem, our data operator, will refresh this once a month. The transform steps must be repeatable ones he can run, not a heroic cleaning exercise that only you can perform.
  • Every number must survive an audit. If the DEO challenges “62% of schools visited,” I want to trace that figure down to the exact rows, which schools, which visits, which duplicates were removed and by what rule. Document every exclusion. A number I cannot defend is worse than no number.
  • Absent children are not zeros. I have seen the ‘AB’ entries. Decide explicitly how absences and unmatched schools are treated, and say so on the dashboard itself, not in a footnote I have to hunt for.

Two habits I am banning outright

No 3D charts. The previous consultant gave us a 3D exploded pie of enrolment. It looked impressive and told us nothing; the tilt made small blocks look smaller and big ones bigger. Flat, honest charts only.
No red-green-only coding. Two of our senior district colleagues are colour-vision deficient. Any status encoding must work without relying on the red/green distinction alone: use position, labels, or a colourblind-safe palette.

A last word on tone: this dashboard will be seen by teachers whose schools appear on it. Design it to prompt support, not shaming. If a school shows poorly because our own data about it is poor, the dashboard should make that distinction visible.

P.S.: Bring me the specification on paper first. I would rather argue over a diagram for an hour than discover a wrong join after the DEO has already quoted the number in a review meeting.

Meera Xalxo
Director, Shiksha Setu Foundation, Ranchi

Deliverables expected in your submission: (1) star-schema data model with grain and keys, (2) plain-language Power Query cleaning plan, (3) 6–10 measure definitions with DAX sketches, (4) a two-page dashboard layout specification with every chart choice justified against this memo.

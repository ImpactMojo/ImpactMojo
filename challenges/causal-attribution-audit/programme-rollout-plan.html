Nirmal Neer Programme: FY 2026–27 Expansion & Rollout Plan

Expansion from 220 to 300 villages · Jhansi, Lalitpur (UP) & Tikamgarh, Chhatarpur (MP)

Version: 2.1 · Prepared by: Head of Operations · Circulated: 12 February 2026 · Status: approved by board, 27 February 2026
This is the operations team’s plan as circulated internally. It was not written with evaluation in mind. Read it alongside the annual report extract: the constraints and logistics described here are what your proposed evaluation design for FY 2026–27 must work within.

1. Scope of the expansion

The programme currently operates in 220 villages. The board has approved expansion to 80 new villages during FY 2026–27 (April 2026 to March 2027), commissioned in four quarterly batches of 20 villages each. Each village receives the full package: source strengthening, household tap connections, water committee formation and twelve monthly hygiene sessions.

2. Village selection: the eligibility shortlist

Field teams have verified 128 eligible villages across 4 blocks (Babina and Talbehat in UP; Palera and Rajnagar in MP). All 128 meet the entry conditions: no functional piped scheme, gram panchayat resolution passed, 5% community contribution committed. Because only 80 can be taken up this year, the shortlist is ranked by the standard need score (0–100), computed identically for every village:

3. Sequencing within the year

Assignment of the 80 selected villages to quarterly batches is operationally flexible. All four blocks have works crews available in every quarter, and no shortlisted village presents a technical reason to be commissioned earlier or later than another. Historically, batch order has been settled informally at the February operations meeting (a mix of contractor convenience and MLA-area requests, which the board has asked us to move away from). A transparent, defensible method for deciding batch order would be welcomed by the field teams.

4. Board position on coverage

“The Trust will not deny or indefinitely delay water to any eligible village for the purpose of research. Every village on the shortlist must receive the programme within the committed horizon. Subject to that, the board encourages the secretariat to strengthen the credibility of our impact evidence, which our two largest funders have questioned.” Board minute 2026/02-07, 27 February 2026

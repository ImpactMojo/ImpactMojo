Where does the CSR money go?

India is one of very few countries that makes corporate charity a legal duty. Section 135 of the Companies Act sends roughly ₹30,000 crore a year into development work. This traces five years of it — which states received it, which causes absorbed it, and how much was never assigned to a state at all.

Money follows income, not need

Each dot is a state: how rich it is across the bottom, how much CSR each resident received up the side. If giving followed need, the dots would slope downward. They do the opposite.

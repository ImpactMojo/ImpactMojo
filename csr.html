Where does the CSR money go?

India is one of very few countries that makes corporate charity a legal duty. Section 135 of the Companies Act sent ₹26,211 crore into development work in 2020-21 and ₹40,794 crore in 2024-25. This traces those five years: which states received the money, which causes absorbed it, and how much was never assigned to a state at all.

Richer states receive more per resident

Each dot is a state, placed by how rich it is across the bottom and how much CSR each resident received up the side. If giving followed need, the dots would slope downward. They do the opposite.

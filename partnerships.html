Our partners

Research & MEL Advisory Partner

Populi

www.populi.co.in ↗

Populi is ImpactMojo’s end-to-end research partner and MEL advisory partner.

In their own description, Populi is “a development advisory firm that partners with foundations, donors, and mission-driven organisations to strengthen the effectiveness of social impact initiatives,” specialising in building data and evidence systems that power decision-making, surface what works, and support real-time course correction.

Populi’s services, as listed on their site

  • MEL framework design
  • Theory of Change development
  • Outcome frameworks
  • Monitoring system design
  • Data collection tools
  • Real-time feedback loops
  • Impact evaluations
  • Process evaluations
  • Developmental evaluations
  • Portfolio evaluations
  • Data analysis & visualisation
  • Learning agendas
  • Needs assessments
  • Evidence reviews
  • Grantmaking strategy
  • Fractional M&E Head

What ImpactMojo does

  • ImpactMojo does capacity building work for Populi.
  • ImpactMojo refers and endorses Populi for full-fledged research projects.

Thought Advocacy & Measurement Partner

India Institute

indiai.org ↗

India Institute is ImpactMojo’s thought advocacy and measurement partner.

In their own description, India Institute is an independent research think tank, established in 2010, that “conducts rigorous social science research in education, critical thinking, and social equity — and translates evidence into policy that expands dignity, choice, and opportunity for individuals across India.”

Their programme areas, as listed on their site

  • Education
  • Critical thinking
  • Measurements
  • Social equity

Their Measurements programme covers five validated instruments for social equity indicators — discrimination, mobility, social capital, mental health and casteist attitudes — alongside a Misinformation Susceptibility Index.

What ImpactMojo does

  • ImpactMojo endorses India Institute and recommends them for measurement work — understanding measurements, tools and validation.
  • ImpactMojo does capacity building work for them.
